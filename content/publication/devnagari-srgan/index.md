abstract: A generative adversarial network with a classifier as a third player that super-resolves low-resolution handwritten Devanagari characters while keeping them classifiable. The generator removes batch normalization and uses residual-in-residual dense blocks; generated images reach an MSE of 0.0507, PSNR of 12.95 dB, SSIM of 0.8172, gradient similarity of 0.9124 and 98% classification accuracy.

summary: An enhanced SRGAN-based approach for super-resolving handwritten Devanagari character images.
